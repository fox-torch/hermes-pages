Hermes — Personal Agent

Hermes is a personal, single-user software agent operated by its developer for the developer's own use. It runs on the developer's own computers and acts as a personal assistant: reviewing schedules, summarizing email, locating files, and carrying out tasks the owner requests.

With the account owner's authorization, Hermes connects to the owner's own Google account through Google APIs in read-only form (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive) and to other services the owner has configured.

This application is not a public service. It is not offered to third parties, accounts are not created for other users, and data accessed through it is used only to provide personal-assistant functions to the account owner.
